WhenceChrome extension

Privacy Policy

Whence does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

Last updated: 2026-10-12.

It has no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of its own. It makes no network requests of its own. Nothing is ever sent off your device.

What it reads, and when

Whence answers "where did this value on the page come from?". To do that it looks at the responses the page you are on fetched for itself (fetch, XMLHttpRequest, JSONP, WebSocket and Server-Sent Events messages).

  • Only after you start it on a tab. Clicking the toolbar icon or using one of its shortcuts starts recording on that tab; before that, nothing is kept. The tab keeps recording, across reloads, until you close it. Which tabs you started is kept by Whence itself (by tab id, in Chrome's session storage for the extension, cleared with the browser session), not by the page: a page can't turn recording on in a tab you didn't start, and a tab it opens or a duplicate of a started tab records nothing. In a tab you did start, the tab is the boundary, not the site: a page you go on to there can ask to be recorded from its first request, as a reload would be.
  • Only in that tab's memory. Text responses (never images, video or downloads), capped at 500 requests / 24 MB plus 1,000 stream messages / 6 MB, discarded when the tab navigates away or closes. Never written to disk, never synced, never transmitted.
  • Not readable by the page. The recording is reachable only by Whence's own interface over a private channel; scripts on the page (including ads and analytics) cannot read it.
  • For one purpose: matching a value you click, or a component you capture, back to the response it came from.

Capture for AI reads the HTML and computed styles of the component you click and, when started from the toolbar or a shortcut, takes a screenshot of the visible tab cropped to that component. The result goes to your clipboard or a file you download. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

  • activeTab — act on the tab you invoked Whence on (toolbar or shortcut), including the Capture for AI screenshot (captureVisibleTab).
  • scripting — on a tab that was open before Whence was installed or updated, show the Whence panel with its "reload this tab" button, and leave that tab a hint before reloading it so it records from its first request. Used only on the tab you invoked Whence on.
  • storage — remember your theme, language, the panel position and a copy counter for a one-time review prompt (on your device; theme and language sync with your Chrome profile), and, for the browser session only, which tabs you started Whence on.

Whence's content scripts are declared on all http(s) pages because the recorder must be in place before a page sends its first request; until you start Whence on a tab it only passes requests through.
