Privacy Policy

Vectorsmith does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no remote servers.

Everything Vectorsmith does happens locally in your browser. Tracing runs entirely on your device: the image you pick is read into a canvas, converted to SVG by the bundled engine in a background worker inside the extension's own hidden frame (so the page you are on stays responsive), and handed back to you as a download or a clipboard copy. No image, no page content, and no derived data ever leaves your device.

There is no network request of any kind in the tracing path. The extension bundles its whole engine — nothing is fetched at runtime.

Permissions

Each permission exists only to serve the single stated purpose:

  • activeTab — reads the current tab only when you invoke Vectorsmith (toolbar button or keyboard shortcut), so it can find the images and inline SVG on the page you are looking at, and capture the visible tab when a logo is cross-origin or drawn to a canvas.
  • scripting — injects the tracing panel into that tab when you open it.
  • storage — remembers your own settings (trace mode, tolerance, export preferences, panel size and position). chrome.storage.sync holds preferences so they follow your Chrome profile; chrome.storage.local holds the panel's on-screen position. The developer has no access to either.

Vectorsmith requests no host permissions and declares no content script. The panel is injected under activeTab, which Chrome grants only at the moment you click — so the extension has no standing access to any site you visit.

Data use

For the Chrome Web Store's data-use declarations, Vectorsmith declares that it does not collect or use any of the listed user-data categories, and it meets all three certifications: no sale of data to third parties, no use for purposes unrelated to the single purpose, and no use to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revision date above changes with it. Any change that affects what the extension does with your data will ship alongside the extension update that introduces it.
